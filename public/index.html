FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Zudo work without internet? expand_more
Yes. Zudo is built from the ground up for Nigeria's unique infrastructure reality. It works fully offline, stores every transaction locally on the device, and syncs automatically to the cloud when internet is restored. No data loss. No downtime.
How do crypto payments work? expand_more
You configure a wallet address per currency (BTC, ETH, USDT, etc.) in your settings. When a customer wants to pay with crypto, Zudo generates a scannable QR code containing the wallet address and exact amount. The customer scans it with any crypto wallet app, sends the payment, and you confirm it in the system. All transactions are recorded in your sales history.
What makes Zudo different from other POS systems? expand_more
Three things: (1) We're built specifically for the Nigerian market — offline-first, power-aware, and optimized for local payment methods. (2) Our interface is designed for speed — a transaction takes under 3 seconds. (3) We offer crypto payment support built-in, giving your business an edge with the growing crypto-conscious customer base. Plus, we include free data migration from any existing system.
Can I use Zudo on multiple devices? expand_more
Absolutely. Zudo works on any device with a browser — desktop, tablet, or phone. All devices sync in real-time so your team stays on the same page.
What payment methods are supported? expand_more
Cash, Bank Transfer, POS terminals, USSD, QR codes, Card payments (Verve, Mastercard, Visa), and Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, USDC, BNB, Solana). All transactions are reconciled automatically in your dashboard — no manual entry required.
